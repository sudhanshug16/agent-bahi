# Annual Income-Tax Compliance Matrix

**Research cutoff:** 21 August 2026
**Status:** research baseline only; not legal advice and not implementation.

This matrix records the official-source baseline for annual income-tax filing, computation, and reporting obligations under the Income-tax Act 2025 and Income-tax Rules 2026. It covers entity-neutral tax audit thresholds, return-form selection, advance-tax obligations, and tax-computation principles. It separates **CONFIRMED OFFICIAL FACT**, **PRODUCT VALIDATION POLICY**, **TENANT CONFIGURATION**, and **OPEN RESEARCH**. A blank or open cell is not permission to infer a rule.

## PRODUCT VALIDATION POLICY

These are product rules for a deterministic annual tax compliance workflow. They are not claims that official sources prescribe a particular software design.

| Policy | Required behavior | Silent failure prevented |
|---|---|---|
| Period-aware rule selection | Freeze the financial year, tax year, governing Act/rule version, entity type, income type, and audit applicability for each return. Select applicable return form from Rule 164/Notification 22 for the frozen year. | A new-law form or rule being applied to an old-law year, or conflicting rule versions being mixed in one return |
| Audit applicability determination | Compute business/professional turnover or gross receipts for the financial year; check against s63 threshold and applicable exemptions. If s63 audit is required, retain audit report linkage; if not, check Rule 164 applicability. | A return being filed without determining whether audit is required, or an audit exemption being overlooked |
| Entity-type and income-type scoping | Determine entity type (individual, HUF, partnership, company) and income composition (salary, business, rental, capital gain, etc.) for the year. Select form and audit scope accordingly; do not assume all taxpayers use the same form. | Wrong form being selected for the entity/income combination |
| Separate return preparation, filing, and acceptance | Track return preparation/upload, filing/submission, portal acceptance, and ARN issuance as separate states. Do not assume filing success from upload success. | Upload success being mistaken for filed status or acceptance |
| No unsupported computation assumptions | Do not hard-code s63 thresholds, advance-tax installment dates, depreciation rates, tax-relief amounts, or form structure without effective-dated official source. | Incorrect tax liability computed from stale or unsupported assumptions |
| Form 26 linkage and reconciliation | Retain Form 26 computation (total income, tax before relief, tax credit, net tax) as separate artifact; reconcile total income and net tax between Form 26 and return form. | A return with mismatched income or tax that was not caught before filing |
| Evidence-linked audit trail | Link every return, audit report, filing receipt (ARN), and tax payment to the frozen financial year and entity. Retain portal acknowledgement and payment evidence. | A return or payment that cannot be reconciled to the financial year or portal record |

## TENANT CONFIGURATION: hard review/block inputs

The following facts are mandatory before a deterministic annual-tax return can be prepared and filed:

- **Entity identity and type**: Tenant legal entity name, PAN, entity form (individual, HUF, partnership, company), and registration status;
- **Applicable rule version and effective dates**: Tax year, financial year, governing Act (2025 or 1961), corresponding Rules (2026 or applicable old-law version) with effective dates;
- **Income type and composition**: All sources of income (salary, business, professional, rental, interest, capital gains, foreign income, etc.) for the financial year;
- **Business/professional turnover or gross receipts**: Total value for the financial year; required to determine s63 audit applicability;
- **Tax audit requirement**: Whether s63 statutory audit is required or Rule 164 audit applies; if yes, audit report status and linkage;
- **Return form selection**: Applicable form from Rule 164/Notification 22 for the entity/income type and year;
- **Tax computation inputs**: Gross income, allowable deductions/expenses, depreciation, tax relief (s87, s89, foreign tax credit), TDS credit, advance-tax payments, regime selection (old/new), and carryforward losses (if applicable);
- **Filing credentials and workflows**: PAN, e-Filing portal access, DSC or authorized signatory method, and TAN (if applicable for business/professional income);
- **Prior-year linkage**: Carryforward losses, unrealized depreciation, and any prior-year tax adjustments or litigation affecting the current year.

Do not infer audit applicability from entity form alone, assume a tax-relief amount without verification, or mark a return accepted from upload success.

## OPEN RESEARCH

The following items must remain visible rather than being filled with unsupported assumptions:

1. **Section 63 statutory audit threshold (current)**: Official Notification 22 and section 63 specification must confirm the current financial-year threshold for individual/HUF, partnership, and company. Current status: OPEN.

2. **Return form selection and structure (Rule 164/Notification 22)**: The applicable return form code and part structure (sections, schedules, mandatory fields) for each entity type and income combination must be extracted from official Notification 22 and Form Navigator. Current status: OPEN.

3. **Advance-tax installment due dates (sections 404/408)**: Official Notification 24 or applicable rule must specify the current advance-tax installment due dates and payment schedule for the financial year. Current status: OPEN.

4. **Depreciation rates by asset class (section 33)**: Official depreciation schedule (Notification 23 or applicable rule) must specify rates for buildings, plant/machinery, vehicles, and intangible assets. Current status: OPEN.

5. **Form 26 filing requirement and structure**: Official Form 26 instruction kit must specify whether Form 26 is filed with the portal return or retained for audit reference, and the exact computation steps and field mapping. Current status: OPEN.

6. **Tax-relief credit rules (sections 87, 89, foreign tax credit)**: Exact computation and eligibility rules for each relief; interaction with regime selection; carry-forward of unused relief. Current status: OPEN.

7. **TDS and advance-tax credit and reconciliation**: Rules for crediting TDS collected during the year and advance-tax paid against the final tax liability; net refund or balance-due computation. Current status: OPEN.

8. **Revised return and amendment workflow**: Whether revised return (ITR-X) or amended return is available under new law; conditions for filing; deadline; authority to amend after filing. Current status: OPEN.

9. **Old-law transition and period handling**: For financial years straddling the old-law and new-law boundary (e.g., partial-year returns), confirm whether blended rules apply or full new law applies. Current status: OPEN.

10. **Portal validation and acceptance timing**: Portal-level validation rules, auto-acceptance criteria, and estimated time for tax department acceptance of filed return. Current status: OPEN.

Until an open item is resolved with an applicable official source and tenant facts, the product must surface an explicit review/block outcome rather than invent a form, rate, due date, or filing status.
