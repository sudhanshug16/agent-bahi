# MCA Companies Act Compliance Matrix

**Research cutoff:** 21 August 2026
**Status:** research baseline only; not legal advice and not implementation.

This matrix records the verified baseline for statutory compliance under the Companies Act 2013 and related MCA regulations. It covers company statutory obligations including financial reporting, board governance, auditor appointment, and Registrar filings. It separates **CONFIRMED OFFICIAL FACT**, **PRODUCT VALIDATION POLICY**, **TENANT CONFIGURATION**, and **OPEN RESEARCH**. A blank or open cell is not permission to infer a rule.

## PRODUCT VALIDATION POLICY

These are product rules for deterministic company statutory compliance. They are not claims that official sources prescribe a particular software design.

| Policy | Required behavior | Silent failure prevented |
|---|---|---|
| Company classification determination | Determine company classification (small, large, holding, OPC, etc.) based on paid-up capital, turnover, and subsidiary status for the financial year. Verify audit applicability from current classification criteria. | Wrong audit requirement, exemption being overlooked, or ineligible company filing with Registrar |
| Audit applicability gate | Verify whether company is subject to mandatory statutory audit per section 139 or applicable exemption notification. Do not assume all companies are exempt; do not assume all are audited. | Audit report missing when required, or unnecessary audit being conducted |
| Form selection from official MCA kit | Select each statutory form (AOC-4, MGT-7, ADT-1, DPT-3, MSME-1, etc.) and required fields from current official MCA form instruction kit; do not infer field requirements from prior-year forms or assumptions. | Wrong or obsolete form being filed, or mandatory field being omitted |
| Auditor qualification and rotation compliance | Verify auditor qualifications per section 139(2) and current Schedule IV; check auditor rotation rules (if any) from current notification; verify auditor appointment/rotation evidence. | Ineligible auditor being appointed, or rotation rule being violated |
| AGM and shareholder approval gate | Verify AGM timing and occurrence; link all statutory approvals (accounts, Board report, auditor appointment, dividend) to documented AGM resolution and voting record. Do not assume approval from Board authorization alone. | Accounts or Board report being marked "approved" without documented shareholder AGM approval |
| Sequential filing and precedence | File annual accounts with Registrar only after AGM is held and shareholder approval is documented. Do not file accounts pending AGM. | Accounts being filed before or without AGM approval |
| Separate filing and acknowledgement | Track Registrar filing submission, auto-acknowledgement, and Registrar processing/acceptance as separate events. Do not assume filing success from upload success. | Filing status being marked "accepted" without documented Registrar acknowledgement |
| Evidence-linked audit trail | Link every statutory filing, form submission, AGM resolution, auditor appointment, and Registrar acknowledgement to the frozen financial year and entity. Retain filing receipts and portal evidence. | A statutory obligation or filing that cannot be reconciled to company records and Registrar evidence |
| Obsolete form rejection | Reject any encoding of Form 23AC, 23ACA, or unverified Form DPT-7. If legacy claims arise, replace them with current verified forms (DPT-3, etc.). | Obsolete forms being used or unverified form references persisting in workflow |

## TENANT CONFIGURATION: hard review/block inputs

The following facts are mandatory before deterministic company statutory compliance can be resolved:

- **Company identity and registration**: Tenant legal entity name, CIN (Corporate Identification Number), Company type (private/public), and registration date with Registrar;
- **Financial year and applicable accounting period**: Financial year (1 April to 31 March per Companies Act standard; any exemption from standard FY must be documented with Registrar approval);
- **Company classification**: Classification (small, large, holding, subsidiary, associate, OPC, etc.) determined from paid-up capital, turnover, and shareholding structure for the financial year;
- **Audit applicability**: Whether company is subject to statutory audit under section 139; if exempt, document the exemption notification and conditions;
- **Auditor appointment status and evidence**: Auditor name, qualification credentials (CA/CS or equivalent per Schedule IV), appointment/rotation status, evidence of Board or AGM appointment resolution;
- **Annual accounts and Board report**: Complete Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, auditor report (if applicable), Board Report, and all required supporting statements (Cash Flow, Consolidated accounts if applicable);
- **AGM conduct and approval**: AGM date held within 6 months of FY close; documented shareholder approvals (accounts, Board report, auditor, dividend if any) with voting record and AGM minutes;
- **Filing credentials and workflows**: Registrar e-filing credentials, DSC for authorized signatory (Director, Company Secretary, or authorized person), and current eMCA module access;
- **Prior-year compliance status**: Registrar filing history, audit/filing status of prior years, and any pending compliance actions or objections from Registrar.

Do not infer audit applicability from company size alone without current exemption verification; do not mark a company compliant without documented Registrar filing acknowledgement.

3. **Auditor qualification rules and Schedule IV**: Current Schedule IV (auditor qualifications and disqualifications) and section 139(2) rules must be verified from official MCA guidance or rules notification. Current status: OPEN.

4. **Auditor rotation and time-bar rules**: If auditor rotation is mandated, current maximum-consecutive-term limit and time-bar on re-appointment must be verified from current rules/notification. Current status: OPEN.

5. **Board Report mandatory disclosures (Schedule VI and notifications)**: Current Schedule VI and all applicable notifications (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, dividend policy, nomination/remuneration committee details, management discussion, etc.) must be verified and integrated. Current status: OPEN.

6. **Consolidated financial statement scope and format**: For holding companies, exact scope of consolidation, elimination rules per current Accounting Standards (Ind-AS), and filing format must be verified. Current status: OPEN.

7. **Audit Committee applicability and composition rules**: Current Audit Committee applicability threshold, mandatory director-composition rules, independence criteria, and meeting frequency must be verified from rules 6-7 and current notifications. Current status: OPEN.

8. **Annual accounts filing format and e-form specification**: MCA's current e-form kit and eMCA module field specifications for all statutory forms (AOC-4, MGT-7, ADT-1, DPT-3, MSME-1, etc.) must be verified. Current status: OPEN.

9. **Auditing and Assurance Standards (current suite)**: Current auditing standards (SA, UASG) applicable to statutory audits must be verified; auditor report format and qualified-vs.-unqualified opinion criteria per current standards. Current status: OPEN.

Current status: OPEN.
